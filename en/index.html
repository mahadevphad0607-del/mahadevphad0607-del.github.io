Training-free Light-Field and Integral-Imaging 3D Reconstruction

4 first-author papers
  • RIM Ray-based inverse mapping redistributes angular samples to spatial positions as a bijection with zero quantization error under orthographic conditions. On 7 Stanford Lytro scenes: PSNR 23.31 → 25.70 dB (+2.39 dB), SSIM 0.741 → 0.877, downstream YOLO detection mAP50 0.665 → 0.776 (+0.11). (ICMV 2026)
  • iRIM The inverse direction: synthesizes the elemental image of a virtual lens array from multi-view images, sized to a display's specification. (ICCAS 2026)
  • Origin-point optimization Moving the reconstruction origin reduces quantization error in volumetric computational reconstruction (ITC-CSCC 2025). Replacing the iterative search with a direct camera-parameter computation cut the time from 25.45 s to 0.31 s on CPU (9×9 elemental images) and 0.0057 s on GPU (CuPy, RTX 3090), with SSIM identical to the iterative method (ITC-CSCC 2026).

Python, NumPy, CuPy, OpenCV · PSNR, SSIM and mAP evaluation

GitHub · origin-point-optimization-vcr (code for the ITC-CSCC 2025 and 2026 papers)
